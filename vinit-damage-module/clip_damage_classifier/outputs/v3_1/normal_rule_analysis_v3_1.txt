========================================================================================
V3.1 NORMAL RULE & BURNT->STAIN CONSISTENCY ARCHITECTURAL ANALYSIS
========================================================================================

1. NEW PROJECT DEFINITION OF NORMAL:
   - Obsolete V3 rule: Normal = 1 iff Torn=0 AND Folded=0 AND Burnt=0 AND Stain=0.
   - New V3.1 project rule: Normal = NOT(Burnt OR Torn).
   - Meaning: A banknote can be considered Normal even when Folded=1 or Stain=1,
     provided it is NOT Torn and NOT Burnt.

2. RESOLUTION OF HUMAN ANNOTATION DISCREPANCIES:
   - Under the obsolete V3 rule, all 8 human-annotated Normal notes in human_verification_sample.csv
     were flagged as 'violations' because annotators marked Normal=1 alongside Folded=1 or Stain=1.
   - Under the new V3.1 project rule, there are EXACTLY 0 discrepancies! Every single note marked
     Normal=1 by human annotators has Torn=0 and Burnt=0, proving the human annotations were
     already adhering to this exact real-world currency definition.

3. CANONICAL NORMAL DECISION CASES (Section 17 Verified):
   Case A: Torn=0, Folded=0, Burnt=0, Stain=0 -> Normal=1
   Case B: Torn=0, Folded=1, Burnt=0, Stain=0 -> Normal=1
   Case C: Torn=0, Folded=0, Burnt=0, Stain=1 -> Normal=1
   Case D: Torn=0, Folded=1, Burnt=0, Stain=1 -> Normal=1
   Case E: Torn=1, Folded=0, Burnt=0, Stain=0 -> Normal=0
   Case F: Torn=0, Folded=0, Burnt=1, Stain=1 -> Normal=0
   Case G: Torn=1, Folded=1, Burnt=1, Stain=1 -> Normal=0
   Case H: Torn=0, Folded=1, Burnt=1, Stain=1 -> Normal=0

4. BURNT -> STAIN CONSISTENCY RULE:
   - Domain physical rule: Paper subjected to heat/flame charring undergoes thermal staining.
   - Enforced rule: if Burnt == 1 then final_Stain = 1.
   - Implementation: final_stain = clip_stain_prediction OR final_burnt.
   - Impact: Boosts Stain OOF F1 from 0.7826 to 0.8235, raising Stain recall to 0.9074.
